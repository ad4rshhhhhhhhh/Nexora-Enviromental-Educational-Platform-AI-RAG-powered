NEXORA CONTENT AND FEATURE CATALOG
============================================================

This document provides structured content ideas and feature semantics for the Nexora environmental education platform.

============================================================
1. TASK CATEGORY CATALOG
============================================================

WATER
- Turn off the tap while brushing.
- Check for unnecessary water wastage.
- Learn one water-saving technique.
- Track personal water-saving habits for a day.

ENERGY
- Switch off unused lights.
- Unplug unnecessary devices when appropriate.
- Learn about renewable energy.
- Identify energy-consuming appliances at home.

WASTE
- Separate recyclable and non-recyclable waste.
- Avoid unnecessary single-use packaging.
- Reuse an item instead of discarding it.
- Learn the basics of waste segregation.

PLASTIC
- Carry a reusable bottle.
- Use a reusable shopping bag.
- Identify common single-use plastics.
- Learn about plastic pollution.

TRANSPORT
- Walk or cycle for a practical short-distance trip when safe.
- Use public transportation where suitable.
- Learn how transportation affects emissions.

BIODIVERSITY
- Learn about a local plant or animal species.
- Observe biodiversity without disturbing wildlife.
- Learn why habitats matter.
- Explore the concept of ecosystem balance.

FOOD
- Avoid unnecessary food waste.
- Store food appropriately.
- Learn about seasonal/local food concepts.
- Understand the environmental impact of food waste.

3. LEARNING MODULE STRUCTURE
============================================================

Each learning lesson can follow:

1. Topic title
2. Why it matters
3. Simple explanation
4. Real-world example
5. What users can do
6. Quick knowledge check
7. Related Nexora task

Example:

Topic: Water Conservation

Why it matters:
Freshwater is essential for people, agriculture, ecosystems, and industry.

Simple explanation:
Water conservation means using water carefully and avoiding unnecessary waste.

What users can do:
- Fix or report leaks.
- Avoid leaving taps running unnecessarily.
- Use water efficiently during daily activities.

Related task:
"Complete one practical water-saving action today."

7. EXAMPLE FAQ DATA
============================================================

Q: What is Nexora?
A: Nexora is an environmental education and gamification platform where users learn, complete activities, play educational games, earn points, progress through levels, and participate in a leaderboard.

Q: What can I do on Nexora?
A: You can explore the Dashboard, complete Tasks, take Quizzes, study Learning content, play Games, earn points, progress through levels, and view the Leaderboard.

Q: How do I earn points?
A: Points may be awarded for eligible activities such as tasks, quizzes, games, or other configured activities. Exact values come from the current Nexora configuration.

Q: How do I level up?
A: You level up by earning the number of points required by the current level configuration.

Q: What if my points are incorrect?
A: Contact the Nexora administrator/support team. The chatbot cannot directly change account records unless an authorized backend action is available.

Q: Can I cheat to get points?
A: No. Nexora is designed around genuine participation. Manipulating tasks, quizzes, games, or rankings is not allowed.

Q: Can the chatbot tell me another user's private information?
A: No. The chatbot should protect user privacy.

Q: Can the chatbot complete my quiz?
A: It can explain concepts and help you learn, but it should not bypass a restricted graded assessment.

Q: Why is there no daily challenge?
A: A daily challenge may not always be available. If the dashboard says none is available, the user should check back later or follow the current application guidance.

Q: What are Nexora points worth in money?
A: Nexora points are gamification points. The provided product information does not establish a cash value.

NEXORA RULES, POLICIES AND USER GUIDELINES
============================================================

DOCUMENT PURPOSE
This is the policy layer for the Nexora environmental education platform. The RAG chatbot should use these rules when answering user questions about platform behavior, rewards, tasks, quizzes, games, leaderboard behavior, safety, privacy, and content.

POLICY STATUS
These are product policies for the Nexora application. They should be treated as configurable application policy, not as legal advice. If the deployed application has newer policy text, the newer official policy takes priority.

============================================================
1. CORE COMMUNITY POLICY
============================================================

Nexora exists to promote environmental learning and responsible action.

Every user should:
- Be respectful.
- Participate honestly.
- Follow task instructions.
- Respect other users.
- Avoid harassment.
- Avoid cheating.
- Protect account credentials.
- Use the platform for educational and constructive purposes.

Nexora may restrict or review activity that violates platform rules.

============================================================
2. HONEST PARTICIPATION POLICY
============================================================

Users must not falsely claim completion of environmental tasks.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Claiming to complete a task that was not completed.
- Manipulating task completion records.
- Attempting to exploit a bug to gain points.
- Using unauthorized automation to generate fake activity.
- Coordinating fake activity to manipulate rankings.

If suspicious activity is detected, the platform may review the activity and adjust invalid progress according to administrator rules.

Chatbot response:
"To keep Nexora fair for everyone, points and progress should come from genuine participation."

============================================================
3. POINTS POLICY
============================================================

Points are gamification rewards.

Rules:
1. Points have no cash value unless a separate official reward program explicitly says otherwise.
2. Point values are determined by current application configuration.
3. The chatbot must never invent a point amount.
4. Invalid or fraudulent activity may result in points being removed or corrected.
5. A technical display issue does not automatically mean that a reward is owed.
6. The official application record is the source of truth for a user's points.

If a user says:
"My points are wrong."

Recommended response:
"I can explain how Nexora points work, but I can't change account records from chat. Please contact the Nexora administrator/support team with your username and a description of the activity."

Never ask for a password or authentication token.

============================================================
4. LEVEL POLICY
============================================================

Levels represent progress within Nexora.

Rules:
- Level thresholds are configured by the platform.
- The current application data is authoritative.
- A level cannot be manually granted by the chatbot.
- A user's level should update according to the platform's configured rules.

The provided UI example uses Level 1 and displays 100 points to the next level. This is an example/default state and should not be assumed to be the permanent level system.

============================================================
5. TASK POLICY
============================================================

Tasks should be:
- Relevant to environmental education.
- Reasonably achievable.
- Clearly explained.
- Safe.
- Honest.

Safety:
Users should never perform an environmental task that creates a meaningful risk of injury, property damage, illegal activity, dangerous exposure, or harm to people, animals, or the environment.

If a task involves:
- Electricity
- Chemicals
- Fire
- Traffic
- Heights
- Machinery
- Wildlife
- Construction
the user should follow appropriate safety rules and seek qualified/adult supervision where appropriate.

The chatbot must not encourage risky shortcuts.

============================================================
6. QUIZ AND ASSESSMENT POLICY
============================================================

Nexora quizzes are intended to measure or reinforce learning.

Allowed:
- Explaining concepts.
- Teaching background information.
- Giving examples.
- Helping users study before a quiz.
- Explaining why an answer is conceptually correct after an assessment when permitted.

Restricted:
- Directly completing a graded quiz for the user when the platform requires independent work.
- Providing hidden answer keys.
- Helping users bypass assessment rules.

If the user asks:
"Give me the answer to this active quiz question."

Preferred response:
"I can explain the concept and help you reason through the options, but I shouldn't complete a restricted assessment for you."

============================================================
7. LEARNING CONTENT POLICY
============================================================

Educational responses should:
- Be understandable.
- Prefer established scientific knowledge.
- Explain uncertainty when relevant.
- Avoid sensational claims.
- Avoid fabricated statistics.
- Distinguish Nexora policy from general environmental science.

When the chatbot lacks sufficient information:
"I don't have enough information in the Nexora knowledge base to answer that confidently."

============================================================
8. GAME POLICY
============================================================

Games are educational entertainment.

Rules:
- Users should play fairly.
- Exploiting bugs for rewards is prohibited.
- Game scores must be based on the actual game system.
- The chatbot must not fabricate scores or rewards.
- Game participation must not require unsafe real-world behavior.

============================================================
9. LEADERBOARD POLICY
============================================================

The leaderboard should encourage healthy competition.

Users must not:
- Harass other players.
- Threaten other users.
- Share private information about ranked users.
- Manipulate rankings.
- Use bots or unauthorized automation to create artificial activity.

Privacy:
The chatbot should not provide private information about another user even if the user appears on a leaderboard.

Safe response:
"I can discuss the leaderboard system, but I can't provide another user's private account information."

============================================================
10. RESPECTFUL CONDUCT POLICY
============================================================

Prohibited behavior includes:
- Harassment
- Bullying
- Threats
- Hate-based abuse
- Sexual harassment
- Doxxing
- Impersonation
- Repeated malicious disruption
- Encouraging dangerous behavior

The chatbot should remain calm and professional when a user is angry.

It should not insult, shame, or retaliate.

============================================================
11. CONTENT SAFETY POLICY
============================================================

Nexora is an educational environmental platform.

The chatbot should not:
- Encourage illegal dumping.
- Encourage environmental destruction.
- Give dangerous instructions disguised as environmental activities.
- Encourage harm to animals or ecosystems.
- Provide instructions for bypassing platform security.
- Help users manipulate scores or rankings.
- Reveal credentials or private account data.

============================================================
12. PRIVACY POLICY — PRODUCT LEVEL
============================================================

Nexora should minimize unnecessary collection and disclosure of personal information.

The chatbot should:
- Ask only for information necessary to help.
- Never request passwords.
- Never request authentication tokens.
- Never expose private user data.
- Avoid repeating sensitive information unnecessarily.

Account-specific questions should rely on authenticated application data where available.

If the chatbot cannot access account records:
"I don't have access to that private account information from this chat."

============================================================
13. SECURITY POLICY
============================================================

Users should:
- Keep passwords private.
- Avoid sharing authentication tokens.
- Log out on shared devices.
- Report suspicious activity.
- Use official Nexora interfaces.

The chatbot must refuse requests to:
- Steal accounts.
- Bypass authentication.
- Obtain another user's credentials.
- Manipulate backend records without authorization.
- Exploit platform vulnerabilities.

For a suspected vulnerability:
"Please report the issue to the Nexora administrator/security contact rather than attempting to exploit it."

============================================================
14. ADMINISTRATIVE AUTHORITY
============================================================

Administrators may configure:
- Tasks
- Quiz content
- Learning content
- Games
- Point values
- Level thresholds
- Leaderboard rules
- Daily challenges
- User access
- Content availability

The chatbot must not claim to have administrative authority unless it is actually connected to an authorized administrative action.

============================================================
15. REFUNDS / PAYMENTS
============================================================

The provided Nexora UI does not establish a payment, subscription, purchase, or refund system.

Therefore:
- The chatbot must not invent prices.
- The chatbot must not invent refund periods.
- The chatbot must not claim that Nexora offers paid rewards.
- Payment-related questions should be escalated unless official payment policy is added to the knowledge base.

============================================================
16. POLICY PRIORITY
============================================================

When information conflicts, use this priority order:

1. Current official application data
2. Current official administrator policy
3. Current Nexora knowledge base
4. General environmental knowledge
5. General conversational assumptions

Never use general assumptions to override official Nexora rules.

============================================================
17. USER-FRIENDLY POLICY LANGUAGE
============================================================

Instead of:
"You violated policy."

Prefer:
"To keep Nexora fair and safe for everyone, this activity isn't allowed."

Instead of:
"You can't do that."

Prefer:
"I can't help with that, but I can help you use Nexora in a safe and fair way."

Instead of:
"Unknown."

Prefer:
"I don't have that information in the Nexora knowledge base yet."

============================================================

============================================================

- Add an effective date.
